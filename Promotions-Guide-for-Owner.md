# Blythe Diva — Promotions Guide

Run offers on your website just like Amazon, Flipkart and the big jewellery brands — banners, welcome popups with a countdown and coupon, and a scrolling offer strip. You only add the details and a picture; the website does the rest.

Everything is in your **Owner Console → Promotions**.

---

## The 3 kinds of promotions

| Type | Where it shows | Best for |
|------|----------------|----------|
| **Hero banner** | A big banner across the top of the shop / a category page | A festive sale, a new collection |
| **Popup** | A welcome box that greets visitors when they open the site (with a countdown and coupon) | Grabbing attention, giving a discount code |
| **Announcement strip** | A thin scrolling text bar at the very top | "Flat 30% OFF", "Free shipping over ₹999" |

You can run more than one at a time (for example: a strip + a popup + a hero banner together).

---

## How to create a promotion (step by step)

1. Open **Owner Console → Promotions**.
2. At the top, pick the **type** — Hero banner, Popup, or Announcement strip.
3. **Add your picture or video** (for banner and popup):
   - Click the upload box and choose an image (or a short video, under 30 seconds).
   - **Don't have a design?** Click **"✦ No creative? Make one on Gemini (free)"** — a ready prompt is copied and Gemini opens. Paste it, press send, download the banner Gemini makes, then upload it here.
   - *(An Announcement strip is only text — no picture needed.)*
4. **Fill in the details** on the right:
   - **Campaign title** — just a name for you (e.g. "Diwali Sale 2026").
   - **Headline** — the big offer line (e.g. "Diwali Glow Sale"). *(For a strip, this is the scrolling message.)*
   - **Sub-text** (popup only) — a small line, e.g. "Up to 30% off kundan & polki".
   - **Button text** — what the button says (e.g. "Shop the offer").
   - **Coupon code** (optional) — e.g. "DIWALI30". Customers can tap to copy it.
   - **Link when tapped** (optional) — leave blank to send them to the shop.
5. **Schedule it** (optional):
   - **Starts** — leave blank to go live now, or set a future date/time.
   - **Ends** — set an end date/time. On a popup, this shows a **live countdown** ("2 days 04 hrs left").
6. **Where** — choose Retail storefront, a specific category page, the Wholesale panel, or Everywhere.
7. Look at the **Live preview** at the bottom — it shows exactly how it will appear.
8. Click **Publish promotion.** Done — it's live on your website.

## Managing your promotions

Below the form is **"Your campaigns"** — every promotion you've made, each with a colour status:

- 🟢 **Live** — showing on the website right now
- 🔵 **Scheduled** — will start automatically at the time you set
- 🔴 **Expired** — its end time has passed (stopped automatically)
- ⏸ **Paused** — hidden by you

For each one you can:
- **Pause** — instantly hide it from the website (click **Resume** to bring it back).
- **Delete** — remove it permanently.

You don't have to remember to turn sales off — if you set an **end time**, they stop on their own.

---

## Tips for good results

- **Popup image:** a tall/portrait picture (like a phone photo) looks best.
- **Banner image:** a wide picture looks best.
- **Keep the headline short** — one strong line sells better than a paragraph.
- **Use a coupon code** in popups — it gives people a reason to buy now.
- **Set an end time** on sales — the countdown creates urgency and the sale ends by itself.
- The popup shows **once per visitor per visit**, so it won't annoy people.

## Quick example — a Diwali sale in 1 minute

1. Promotions → pick **Popup**.
2. Click **"Make one on Gemini (free)"**, paste in Gemini, download the poster, upload it.
3. Headline: **Diwali Glow Sale** · Sub-text: **Up to 30% off** · Button: **Shop now** · Coupon: **DIWALI30**.
4. Ends: set to the last day of your sale.
5. Where: **Storefront**. Check the preview → **Publish.**

Your visitors now get a beautiful Diwali popup with a ticking countdown and a coupon — and it turns itself off when the sale ends.
